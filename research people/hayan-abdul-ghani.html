Ministerial Record (2022–2026)

  • Output & exports — Claimed national production ~4 mb/d and exports 3.4–3.5 mb/d (Jun 2024), ~70% of exports to Asia; total export capacity push toward 5 mb/d via Fishkhabour/Baniyas pipelines & new terminals [khbar.net interview; The National]
  • Gas — Gas utilization raised to ~63% (~1,900 mmscfd invested, ~1,000 mmscfd still flared as of Jun 2024); pledge to end routine flaring 2028–2030; goal of gas-import independence [khbar.net]
  • Refining — +360 kb/d refining capacity added during his government; gasoline self-sufficiency target; trial ops at new refineries (Nov 2025) incl. Nasiriyah-area capacity [khbar.net; Iraq Business News]
  • Kirkuk / BP — Oversaw activation of the rehabilitation & development contract for four North Oil Company Kirkuk fields to BP (MoU → activated Oct 2025) [Iraq Business News]
  • Licensing — Launched invitations for the 5th & 6th licensing rounds; noted low US company participation (Mar 2025); open-door stance to US investment [CNBC Arabia]
  • Kurdistan & Türkiye — Negotiations with Ankara and KRG to resume Kirkuk–Ceyhan exports (halted since 2023); Mar 2026: announced northern pipeline (200–250 kb/d, renovated route bypassing KRG territory) could restart within a week [CNBC Arabia; PTJ/LinkedIn; AA]
  • Strait of Hormuz crisis (from Feb 28, 2026) — After Iran's IRGC closed the Strait (Mar 2, 2026) amid the Iran war: Basra production cut ~70% (3.3 → ~0.9–1.3 mb/d); exports fell ~75% (stated 8 Aug 2026); storage full, output redirected to domestic refineries; salary payment delays nationwide. Ministry pivoted to Ceyhan restart, Aqaba route studies, Basra–Haditha pipeline [Reuters 8 Mar 2026; AA 21 Mar 2026; AP/Asharq Al-Awsat; IraqiNews]
  • Tanker fleet — Pushed to secure budget for IOTC to buy its own oil tanker fleet during the export crisis [Kurdistan24/kurdiu]

Political Profile

  • Technocrat-cadre hybrid: career operator promoted into cabinet, not a lifelong politician; technocrat face of a Maliki-bloc quota (State of Law, 3 ministers in Sudani's 2022 cabinet — oil among them) [ORSAM cabinet analysis]
  • 2024–2025: defected politically from Maliki's orbit into PM Sudani's own coalition/list (alongside Basra/Karbala/Wasit governors & MP Alia Nassif) — a move that angered Maliki [Carnegie Dec 2025; The New Region]
  • Kept the "Deputy Prime Minister for Energy Affairs" combined title — elevated rank vs previous oil ministers, reflecting the portfolio's centrality [Reuters; Wikidata]
  • Left office when the Sudani government fell after the Nov 2025 elections and the months-long post-election stalemate; new PM Ali al-Zaidi (40, businessman, Coordination Framework nominee) took power 14 May 2026 [Al Jazeera; Al-Monitor]

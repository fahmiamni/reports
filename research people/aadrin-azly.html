Leadership Philosophy (from interviews)

  • Strategy > execution clarity: "most of the time you trip yourself up during implementation because the strategy is not clear" — a self-described stickler for clarity; strategy should be "simple enough to tell your parents"
  • Delegation style: "surround yourself with smarter people than me… provide the clarity of direction, remove bottlenecks, let the experts execute"
  • Advice to young talent (BeDigital session 2023): a degree opens doors but keep learning; have options in life; passion alone is not enough; INTEGRITY is the most important; don't react to everything — choose when to act
  • Origin story: driven by wanting to leave his hometown and see the world; "I had nothing to lose, so I focused on learning and self-improvement"
